SentinelLink

Emergency Response & Disaster Management Platform

SentinelLink is a web-based emergency response and disaster management prototype designed to help users report incidents, monitor emergencies, analyze data, and coordinate responses through a centralized platform.

The project focuses on improving emergency awareness, reducing response delays, and providing a structured system for managing incidents such as floods, fires, railway emergencies, accidents, and other disasters.

---

🌍 Problem Statement

During emergencies, lack of real-time information, delayed communication, and poor coordination can increase damage and slow down response efforts.

SentinelLink aims to provide a simple digital platform where incidents can be reported, tracked, analyzed, and managed efficiently.
