Help & Support

Find answers, guidance and emergency support for SentinelLink.

Getting Started

SentinelLink is designed to help citizens, emergency responders and government agencies report incidents, monitor disasters and coordinate public safety operations. Begin by logging into your account and navigating to the Dashboard to access all available services.

Quick User Guide

Dashboard

View current incidents, emergency statistics and quick shortcuts.

Report Incident

Submit railway, disaster or medical incidents with location details.

Emergency Map

Monitor shelters, hospitals and active incidents on a live map.

Analytics

Review trends and performance reports for informed decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report an emergency?

Open the Report page, complete the incident form and submit it.

Who receives my report?

Reports are reviewed by authorised administrators and forwarded to the appropriate emergency response teams.

Can I track my report?

Yes. Approved reports can be monitored from the Reports section.

Emergency Contacts

🚑 Medical Emergency

Contact your local emergency medical service immediately in the event of a life-threatening medical emergency.

🚒 Fire & Rescue

Report fires, hazardous material incidents or rescue situations to your local fire service.

👮 Police Assistance

Contact your local police for security incidents, public safety concerns or criminal activity.

🚆 Railway Authorities

Notify the relevant railway control authority immediately if you observe track obstructions, damaged crossings or other railway hazards.

Troubleshooting

I cannot log in.

Verify your username and password, check your internet connection and try again. If the issue persists, contact your system administrator.

My report is not visible.

Recently submitted reports may require verification before appearing in the Reports dashboard.

The map is not updating.

Refresh the page and ensure location services are enabled if using live location features.

Notifications are not arriving.

Check your notification preferences in the Settings page and confirm that device notifications are enabled.

Support

If you require additional assistance, contact your organisation's SentinelLink administrator or technical support team. Include details such as the page being used, the action you were performing and any error messages encountered to help diagnose the issue quickly.

Safety Notice

SentinelLink is designed to support emergency coordination and situational awareness. During a real emergency, always follow instructions issued by authorised emergency services and local authorities. The platform complements official response procedures and should be used responsibly.
