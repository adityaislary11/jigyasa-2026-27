SentinelLink

One Platform. Safer Communities.

Our Mission

SentinelLink is an integrated public safety platform designed to strengthen emergency preparedness, disaster response and railway safety through one unified digital system. The platform enables citizens, emergency services and local governments to collaborate using real-time reporting, live monitoring and data-driven decision making.

The Problem

Railway Safety

Accidents caused by obstacles, damaged crossings and delayed reporting continue to threaten lives and infrastructure.

Natural Disasters

Floods, landslides, fires and severe weather often overwhelm emergency services due to delayed information.

Communication Gaps

Citizens, authorities and response teams frequently rely on disconnected systems, reducing coordination during emergencies.

Our Solution

Live Incident Reporting

Citizens can quickly report emergencies with location and category details.

Interactive Emergency Map

A centralized map helps responders visualize incidents, shelters and critical locations.

Analytics Dashboard

Decision makers gain insights through trends, statistics and operational reports.

Administration Panel

Authorities can verify reports, allocate resources and coordinate response teams efficiently.

Key Features

🚆 Railway Safety

Monitor railway crossings, report hazards and improve response time to reduce accidents.

🌊 Disaster Response

Support emergency response during floods, landslides, fires and other natural disasters.

📍 Live Mapping

Visualise incidents, shelters and critical infrastructure on a unified emergency map.

📊 Analytics

Generate insights using reports, trends and operational statistics to support informed decisions.

👥 Administration

Manage users, verify incidents and coordinate emergency resources from one dashboard.

🔔 Smart Alerts

Deliver timely notifications to citizens and authorities during emergency situations.
